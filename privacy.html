Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

Count on the Farm ("the app") is developed by Liza Ma. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

Short version

Count on the Farm does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no third-party trackers of any kind.

What the app does on your device

The app remembers one setting on your device: whether the narration is in English or Mandarin. This setting never leaves your device, is not visible to us, and is removed if you delete the app.

No internet connection

All pictures, sounds, and narration are built into the app. The app works entirely offline and does not send or receive anything over the internet.

Children's privacy

Count on the Farm is designed to be used by young children together with a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information at all, there is nothing about any user — child or adult — for us to hold, use, or share. The app contains no links that lead out of the app.

Third parties

The app does not integrate any advertising, analytics, or social SDKs, and does not share data with third parties, because it does not collect any data to share.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that involves data — this page will be updated and the effective date above will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to liza@mamabuilds.dev.
