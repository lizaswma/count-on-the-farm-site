Common questions

Does the app need an internet connection?
No. Everything is built into the app, so it works fully offline, including on a plane or in the car.

How do I switch the narration to Mandarin?
Tap the "EN" button in the top-right corner of the home screen. It switches to "中文", and the app remembers your choice.

Is any account or sign-in required?
No. There's no account system — just open the app and play. See the Privacy Policy for details on what data is (and isn't) collected.
